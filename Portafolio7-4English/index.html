ABOUT ME

Fernando Rafael Alvarez

Backend Developer · Java

Hello, my name is Fernando Rafael Alvarez, and I am a backend developer with frontend knowledge and years of experience as a self-taught developer. Before pursuing a career in software development, I worked as a teacher, teaching chemistry, and held positions that required me to work in natural sciences laboratories and with computers. However, one day I decided to change career paths in order to make better use of my technical skills and my ability to create solutions.

This career change was not easy because I had to acquire a great deal of knowledge that was new to me. I had to research topics that are studied at university, such as operating systems, computer networks, databases, UML, etc. However, I can say that I have been successful in acquiring this knowledge.

I am confident that my previous work experience has given me advantages in areas such as soft skills, mentoring, and team leadership.

Some of the programming languages and technologies I have experience with include Java, SQL, Spring Boot, Angular, and Docker, among others.

Another important skill I possess is the ability to understand English, which has been crucial to acquiring knowledge, since a great deal of high-quality information is available in English and is very difficult to find in Spanish.

In this portfolio, you will be able to learn more about the technical skills I have acquired since I started this new career path, as well as some of the projects I have been working on.
